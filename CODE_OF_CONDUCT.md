# Code of conduct

OpenRemoTV follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers and people of every background, and keep
criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and publishing other people's private information
(including their TV's IP address, Wi-Fi name or account details) are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the repository's Security tab ("Report a vulnerability") or to the
maintainer listed on the repository. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits,
issues and contributions that break this code, and may ban repeat offenders.
