OpenRemoTV is a phone remote and debloater for Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV. It talks to your TV over Wi-Fi with ADB, without root.

<b>Remote</b>: arrows, OK, back, home, play, volume, mute, power and typing on the TV, with hold-to-repeat keys.
<b>Apps</b>: open anything on the TV.
<b>Send</b>: play YouTube links, show photos, play videos, or drop any file on the TV. Share to OpenRemoTV from any app.
<b>Sweep</b>: back up first, then turn off bloatware from a list tested on 260 TVs, or the common list. Apps are disabled, never uninstalled, and the ones the TV needs are locked.
<b>Undo anytime</b>: back to your first backup with one tap.
<b>Check</b>: find apps a TV update switched back on.
<b>Home screens</b>: MangaHome, a whole comic page with Remo the mascot, or IndiaMinimalist, with photos of India, the Panchang and festivals.

English and Hindi. No accounts, no analytics, no ads.
